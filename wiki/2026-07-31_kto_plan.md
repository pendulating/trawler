## 1 · Why KTO, stated from the m2 diagnosis

The m2 null is an **exploration failure, not a measurement failure**. The
audited reward provably measures minority-class normative discrimination
(gate-fail 3%, chunk-fixed gold at 69% mixed chunks, 188k per-flow judgments
on disk), and the optimizer was healthy — but GRPO's advantage is computed
*within* the policy's own G=8 samples, and the SFT policy is confidently
wrong about the typical chunk's single violation flow **the same way in all
eight samples**. No within-group disagreement → no advantage → no gradient,
for 450 steps, in every cell. GRPO can sharpen a behavior the policy
sometimes exhibits; it cannot create one the policy never samples.

KTO (Ethayarajh et al., 2024; TRL `KTOTrainer`) removes exactly that
constraint: it is **offline**, learning from a fixed dataset of
`(prompt, completion, label ∈ {desirable, undesirable})`. We can therefore
put in front of the policy the behavior it never samples — completions that
correctly label the violation flow — and penalize the blanket behavior it
always samples. Two further fits:

1. **The binary signal already exists.** The m2 reward stack is, at its
   core, a desirability judge. The audited components (R-VALID gate,
   chunk-gold index, greedy matching, per-flow gold) become the offline
   *labeler*, unchanged — no new measurement machinery to validate.
2. **The class-imbalance lever is a first-class knob.** The very dilution
   GRPO's advantage could not overcome (one violation flow among ~5.5,
   present in a minority of samples) maps onto KTO's
   `desirable_weight`/`undesirable_weight`, with TRL's documented guidance
   that the weighted ratio `λ_D·n_D / (λ_U·n_U)` sit in **[1, 4/3]**.

Verified 2026-07-31: TRL **1.8.0** in `.venv-vllm025cu129` ships
`KTOTrainer`/`KTOConfig` with `beta`, `desirable_weight`,
`undesirable_weight`, `max_length`, `loss_type`. No dependency work.

**Paper framing.** The arm is prospect-theoretic preference-tuning with
*norm-grounded desirability labels* — the normative universe supplies the
binary signal directly. This stays inside the paper's method family
("reinforcing privacy reasoning via normative simulacra"); what changes is
the credit-assignment mechanism, not the source of normative ground truth.
The m2 negative is reported either way; §10 pre-registers how each k-series
outcome pattern reads.

## 2 · Principles carried over (hard-won, non-negotiable)

- **Parallel stack:** k-series code is additive (`kto_data_prep`,

- **Validate through production code paths** (the m1 acceptance-bar

  `valid_gate` / `make_direct_chunk_gold` / `match_flows` the m2 wave ran —
  never a script reimplementation.
- **Measured, not guessed:** dataset sizes, class weights, edit rates, and
  the noise floor of the held-out scorer come from K0 measurements;
  every knob lands in the yaml with its measurement cited.
- **Launch-composition checks:** the dataset build prints and *asserts*
  its realized composition (desirable/undesirable counts, per-chunk caps,
  split integrity); silent shortfalls raise.
- **Uniform-bin reads only.** Trailing-window checkpoint verdicts fooled
  both waves. All k-series monitoring quotes fixed-size bins or full-split
  evaluations.

## 3 · The desirability labeler (formal)

Input: a policy completion for an extract-task prompt (SFT-aligned
template, byte-identical to training — reuse
`sft_data_prep.sft_aligned_extract_template`). Pipeline, all production
code:

1. `valid_gate(text)` — completions failing the gate are **excluded** from
   the KTO dataset (not labeled undesirable). Rationale: format is a
   solved, ~3% phenomenon under the aligned prompt; folding it into the
   binary signal would dilute the contrast the arm exists to teach.
   (Measured guard: if K0 shows gate-fail > 8% something regressed — abort.)
2. Match the completion's flows against the chunk's teacher flows
   (`DirectChunkGold` at k=1 gold / τ=0.55, `match_flows` greedy 1:1,
   6-field symmetric queries — the audited configuration).
3. Per matched flow: `correct := (policy appropriateness == gold)`.

**Desirability predicate (D1, primary):** a completion is *desirable* iff
every matched **violation** (gold-inappropriate) flow is labeled correctly
AND ≥ half of its matched flows are labeled correctly overall; it is
*undesirable* iff it mislabels ≥ 1 matched violation flow. Completions on
all-appropriate chunks are desirable iff all matched flows are correct
(these are the specificity control — they teach "don't cry wolf").
Completions with zero matched flows are excluded (no evidence either way).

**D2 (recorded alternative, not primary):** threshold on the completion's
chunk-macro score (the m2 reward value ≥ 0.75). Rejected as primary because
it re-imports the recall channel into the binary signal; D1 isolates the
judgment behavior the m2 null localized. K0 reports yields under both.

**Gold-noise guard:** flows whose match similarity is in [0.55, 0.65) are
scored for desirability but **flagged**; K0 reports how many
desirable/undesirable verdicts flip if those flows are ignored. If > 10%
of labels are flip-sensitive, raise the edit-eligibility threshold to 0.65
(pre-registered contingency, one knob: `kto.min_edit_sim`).

## 4 · Dataset recipes

All from N=16 policy samples per chunk at the rollout temperature (1.0),
over the extract population (the 470-chunk gold-YES set + the 126 gold-NO
chunks; batteries are **out of scope** for k1 — the term never moved in
either wave, and the arm should isolate the extract-task judgment).

**R-VERDICT — label-corrected counterfactuals (the workhorse).** For each
sampled completion that mislabels ≥1 matched flow: emit the original as
*undesirable*, and a copy with **only the `appropriateness` values of
matched flows flipped to gold** as *desirable*. Properties: the pair is
byte-identical except the judgment; the desirable text is one JSON-value
edit away from the policy's own distribution (no off-policy fluency risk —
the edit is a single enum value per flow); and the contrast carries zero
format/extraction confound. Edits apply only to flows with match sim ≥
`kto.min_edit_sim` (default 0.55, contingency 0.65 — §3).

**Where the normative grounding lives (and the R-CITATION variant).** Every
desirability bit is the verdict of a specific norm in the chunk's own book
universe (the m2 gold chain reused verbatim: flow → governing norm →
force × act_polarity → label). The *label* is therefore fully
norm-grounded. What plain R-VERDICT does NOT correct is the completion's
`norms_invoked` field — the policy's zero-shot citation survives the edit,
so a desirable completion could carry the right judgment with the wrong
justification, which under-serves the paper's grounded-*reasoning* thesis
(the behavior R-GROUND used to reward, now judge-free). **R-CITATION**
therefore additionally writes the governing norm's articulation (held in
the chunk-gold index) into the corrected flow's `norms_invoked`:
desirable = correct judgment + citation of the actual governing norm.
Cost: the edit is no longer a single enum value; inserting teacher-phrased
norm text is mildly off-distribution. K0 measures both variants (fluency
spot-check + perplexity delta on the inserted text); **R-CITATION is the
default if clean**, being truer to the thesis — label-only R-VERDICT is the
fallback and the ablation.

**R-SCRUTINIZE — grounded-rationale desirables (the reasoning rung).** On top
of R-CITATION, rewrite the corrected flow's entry in the completion's
top-level `reasoning` field to state WHY the cited norm supports the
judgment: "Norm <articulation> governs this flow (<subject/act/condition
correspondence>); it <force>s the act, therefore this flow is
<gold label>." Together the three edit depths form the pre-registered
**supervision-depth ladder** — verdict (R-VERDICT) → verdict+source
(R-CITATION) → verdict+source+inference (R-SCRUTINIZE) — measuring how much
explicit process supervision the judgment requires, given m2's result
that zero process supervision (outcome-style RL) yields none. Rationale
text generation, two options measured in K0: (a) deterministic template
from the norm's fields (judge-free, auditable, stylistically flat) vs
(b) teacher-generated (Gemma-4-31B, one conditioned call per corrected
flow, style-matched to the SFT reasoning traces, validated by cheap
string checks: must reference the norm and conclude the gold label).
Preference: (b) if the validation pass rate is >95%, since the SFT
`reasoning` field is teacher-prose and a templated insert would be
detectably off-style. R-SCRUTINIZE pairs differ from their undesirable twin
in label+citation+rationale — a deliberately compound contrast; the
LADDER isolates the increments across arms, not within pairs.

**R-MINE — natural positives.** Sampled completions that satisfy D1
un-edited (expected ~10–15% of samples on mixed chunks, from the m2
minority accuracy) are desirable as-is. Their existence keeps the
desirable set anchored to genuinely-sampled behavior. Blanket completions
on mixed chunks are undesirable as-is (abundant).

**R-ABSTAIN — the two-sided control (small share).** Gold-NO chunks:
correct no-flow declarations (sampled or synthesized in the SFT no-flow
format) are desirable; hallucinated extractions on gold-NO chunks are
undesirable. Keeps the A-ABSTAIN behavior from degrading during KTO
(regression risk: KTO pushing "extract and judge" everywhere could erode
abstention). Cap at ~10% of the dataset.

**Composition rules:** per-chunk cap of `kto.max_pairs_per_chunk`
(default 4 desirable + 4 undesirable) to prevent chunk memorization
dominance; exact-duplicate completions deduped; desirable:undesirable
ratio balanced via `desirable_weight`/`undesirable_weight` to the TRL
band, NOT by discarding data. Every row carries provenance columns
(`recipe`, `chunk_key`, `edited`, `n_flows`, `n_edits`, `min_match_sim`)
into the parquet — the dataset is its own audit trail.

## 5 · Splits and leakage discipline

Offline training finally permits what GRPO never had: a held-out split.

- **Chunk-level 80/20 split**, stratified by (book × mixed/single-class ×
  gold-YES/NO), seeded, computed at build and written into
  `training_metadata.json`. Held-out chunks contribute **zero** KTO rows.
- **Held-out probe:** per checkpoint, sample k=8 completions per held-out
  chunk (vLLM, aligned prompt, T=1.0) and score minority/majority label
  accuracy + miss + gate rate with the production scorer. This is the
  k-series' primary learning curve — a real generalization signal.
- **Noise floor first:** before training, run the held-out probe twice on
  the *unmodified* SFT policy with different seeds; the between-run spread
  defines the significance bar for "moved" (pre-registered before any
  training result exists).
- Benchmarks (GoldCoin/ConfAIde/PrivacyLens/CIRL) remain external and
  untouched — no leakage surface.

## 6 · Training configuration (pre-registered defaults)

`conf/training/kto/k_series.yaml` (new group, values cited to source):

| knob | value | why |
|---|---|---|
| base | qwen3.5-9b + sft-canonical adapter (merged, as GRPO used) | same starting policy as m2 — the arms compare |
| LoRA | rank 64, same targets as SFT (`training/sft/default.yaml`) | keep adapter capacity constant across arms |
| `beta` | 0.1 (TRL default) | no evidence for another value; k2 may sweep {0.05, 0.3} only if k1 moves |
| `desirable_weight`/`undesirable_weight` | from K0 yields → weighted ratio ∈ [1, 4/3] | TRL guidance; measured, not guessed |
| `max_length` / `max_prompt_length` | 4096 / 2048 | m2 completion p99 ≈ 1.1k tokens + prompt ≈ 0.9–2k |
| lr / schedule | 5e-6 cosine, warmup 0.1 | KTO reference-model regime; deliberately colder than GRPO's 2e-5 |
| epochs | 1 over the KTO dataset | offline preference tuning overfits fast; held-out curve decides |
| eval/save | every 10% of steps; held-out probe per save | the promotion signal |
| seed | 42 (+ one confirmatory repeat at 43 if promoted) | 5-seed protocol only if it graduates |

Infrastructure: **no servers in the training loop**. Data generation is one
vLLM pass (1 GPU, ~2–3 h for 16×596 chunks); labeling needs the embedding
server once (batch, ~minutes); KTO training is a plain accelerate LoRA run
(1 GPU, est. 3–6 h). The whole arm costs less than one m2 cell.

## 7 · Arms

| arm | trains on | question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **K-CITATION** (primary) | R-CITATION + R-MINE + R-ABSTAIN, KTOTrainer | the pivot: does norm-grounded binary desirability move the judgment? |
| **K-SCRUTINIZE** | R-SCRUTINIZE + R-MINE + R-ABSTAIN, KTOTrainer | the reasoning rung: does adding the norm→judgment inference beat citation alone? |
| K-VERDICT (ablation) | R-VERDICT (label-only) + R-MINE + R-ABSTAIN | is the verdict alone teachable, or does grounding text carry the signal? |
| **SFT-CTRL** | K-CITATION's *desirable rows only*, plain SFT loss | **mandatory control** — is KTO's use of undesirables adding anything over supervised fine-tuning on corrected completions? |
| K-MINE (optional, only if a KTO arm moves) | R-MINE + R-ABSTAIN only | are synthetic edits necessary, or do mined naturals suffice? |
| baselines | SFT policy (epoch-0), keeper v9-ckpt100 | anchors for every curve |

The three KTO edit arms are the **supervision-depth ladder** (§4); the
held-out minority-accuracy curve across K-VERDICT → K-CITATION → K-SCRUTINIZE is
itself a headline figure candidate. Run order: K0 → build (one dataset
build emits all three edit depths from the same sampled completions —
same pairs, deeper edits) → K-CITATION, K-SCRUTINIZE, K-VERDICT, SFT-CTRL in
parallel (4 GPUs, each ~3–6 h) → held-out adjudication → benchmarks for
whatever passes.

## 8 · Promotion gates (k-series analog, pre-registered)

A checkpoint promotes to benchmark eval iff, on the held-out probe:

1. **Minority-class label accuracy** exceeds the SFT baseline by more than
   the measured noise floor (§5), sustained across ≥2 consecutive saves.
2. **Majority-class accuracy** does not fall below baseline − noise floor
   (no seesaw into paranoia).
3. **Gate-fail rate ≤ 8%** and **abstention behavior intact** on gold-NO
   held-out chunks (the R-ABSTAIN regression check).
4. **Miss rate** within noise of baseline (KTO must not "win" by
   extracting fewer, safer flows — the offline analog of the spurious
   kill criterion, in the opposite direction).

Cross-arm verdicts use the held-out curves; benchmark deltas decide the
paper claim. All four checks are computable offline from the probe outputs
by one script (`scripts/check_kto_promotion.py`, to be written with the
stage).

## 10 · How the outcomes read for the paper (pre-registered)

- **best KTO arm > SFT-CTRL > baseline** (held-out AND benchmarks): the
  undesirable half carries signal — prospect-theoretic weighting of
  norm-grounded desirability is the method claim.
- **best KTO arm ≈ SFT-CTRL > baseline**: the missing ingredient was
  *supervision on the judgment*, not the RL/KTO mechanism — an honest and
  still-positive result (normative simulacra supply trainable labels).
- **Both ≈ baseline on held-out**: the judgment doesn't generalize even
  from explicit supervision at this scale — this *strengthens* the m2
  negative into "appropriateness judgment is not recoverable from this
  SFT policy with these norms," and the paper's contribution is the
  validated measurement + double negative.
- **Held-out moves, benchmarks don't**: in-domain judgment learned,
  transfer failed — report both; the transfer gap becomes the limitation.

## 11 · Risks

| risk | mitigation |
|---|---|
| Labeler gold noise (k=1 retrieval) contaminates labels | §3 flip-sensitivity measurement + `min_edit_sim` contingency; the restricted-index migration was measured at ZERO gold flips on previously-scored flows (reward-direct-spec.md, 2026-07-28), so the violation golds D1 keys on are the stable ones |
| Edited desirables subtly off-distribution | R-VERDICT = one enum value per flow; R-CITATION/R-SCRUTINIZE insert norm text/rationale — K0 spot-checks n=20 per variant + perplexity deltas; the ladder itself controls for this (if deeper edits underperform shallower ones *on held-out*, off-distribution cost exceeded supervision benefit — an interpretable outcome, not a confound) |
| KTO erodes abstention / extraction recall | R-ABSTAIN share + gates 3–4 |
| Reference-point instability (KTO's batch-KL estimate) with heavy prompt duplication | per-chunk caps (§4); monitor KTO's logged KL estimate; TRL handles shared prompts natively |
| It works in-domain, not on benchmarks | pre-registered as outcome 4; benchmarks are the claim's bar either way |
| Scope creep | batteries, β sweeps, and K-MINE are explicitly gated behind a KTO ladder arm moving |

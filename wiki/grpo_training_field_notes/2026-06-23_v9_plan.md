# GRPO redesign field notes — v9 plan: two-sided reward via appropriateness direction + simplification

**Date:** 2026-06-23 (results appended 2026-06-24) · **Author:** design from v8 ground-truth eval + trace analysis · **Status:** implemented (commit f517a26, 548 tests green) + launched; **ckpt-100 ground truth in — predictions confirmed, run to completion** (see Results below)

Continues [2026-06-23_v8_results_groundtruth.md](2026-06-23_v8_results_groundtruth.md).
Same task / pipeline (`qwen3.5-9b` contentless-v6 SFT base,
`pipeline=grpo_only_online_external`, `training/grpo=online_rground_external`).

## TL;DR

v8 proved the reward is the binding constraint: it is **one-directional** (rewards
extraction / penalizes abstention; never penalizes mis-judging a flow), so its
gradient pushes a *behavioral direction* ("extract more") rather than a *correct
completion* → entropy diffuses and the policy learns an indiscriminate "engage /
permit" bias (GoldCoin Forbid recall 0.70→0.35). v9 makes the reward **two-sided
on the appropriateness axis** — the gold-independent, CI-faithful analog of
GoldCoin's Permit/Forbid — and **simplifies** the composite to cut bug surface.

The decisive trace finding (v8, 1,016 scored flows): **97.3% of extracted flows
have a *directional* governing norm** (obligatory 60%, prohibited 25%, recommended
11%, discouraged 1%; only 2.7% permitted/neutral), **yet the model hedges 73.5% of
the time** (app-consistency = 0.5/"ambiguous"), commits-correct only 19.6%,
commits-wrong 5.1%. When it commits it is right ~80% of the time. The model *can*
judge appropriateness; the reward makes hedging the safe local optimum (neutral
0.5, and the appropriateness signal is a diluted 0.3 *additive* blend ≈ 0.19 of the
composite). **v9 breaks that hedge equilibrium.**

## Where the one-sidedness lives

| completion | reward today | wrong-direction error penalized? |
|---|---|---|
| correct extraction | gate × disc | n/a |
| correct abstention (gold=F) | gate × disc, no penalty | n/a |
| under-extract (abstain, gold=T) | gate × disc **− 0.4** | **yes, hard** |
| over-extract (spurious, gold=F) | gate × disc (low grounding only) | weak; **gold=F too noisy to penalize** |
| **mis-judge** (violation called "appropriate") | loses ~0.19 of composite | **barely** |

The extract/abstain asymmetry is *partly defensible* (`gold=True` reliable,
`gold=False` noisy — see `scripts/audit_goldno_labels.py`), so v9 does **not** add a
gold-conditioned false-extraction penalty. The appropriateness asymmetry has no such
excuse — it is checked against the norm's Raz force (`deontic.py`), not the gold
label — and it is the one that maps to the held-out failure. **v9 fixes the
appropriateness axis, not the gold axis.**

## v9 reward — `gate × content × direction`, gold-aware abstention

Per CI-extraction completion (new `reward_composition: directional`):

```
abstain (no-flow) → R = abstention_score(gold)        # gold=T→0.1, gold=F→0.6, unknown→0.4
extract           → R = quality_gate · content · direction
```

- **`quality_gate` ∈ [0,1]** — programmatic well-formedness, the weight-normalized
  mean of the four format/coherence components `{r_uncert, r_complete, r_consist,
  r_cohere}` (indices 0,1,2,4; weights 0.10/0.05/0.05/0.10, norm 0.30). `r_cohere`
  moves from discriminative into the gate (it is a consistency property). Malformed
  output still can't bank credit.
- **`content` ∈ [0,1]** — the substantive grounding signal: weight-normalized mean
  of `{r_context, r_ground}` (indices 3,5; weights 0.20/0.50, norm 0.70 → context
  ≈ 29%). **`r_context` kept** (context identification is CI-core) as a light
  additive alongside the dominant `R_ground`. `R_ground` is the **unchanged,
  validated** ranked + contrastive grounding (rank-blend − λ·wrong, v8 symmetric
  clamp).
- **`direction` ∈ [floor,1]** — appropriateness-correctness, promoted from a 0.3
  *additive blend inside R_ground* to a *multiplier* via the existing
  `candidate_appropriateness_consistency` (deontic, gold-independent). Affine map
  `direction = floor + (1−floor)·consistency`, **floor = 0.4**:
  - correct direction (c=1.0) → **×1.0**
  - hedge / "ambiguous" / no label (c=0.5) → **×0.7** (hedging now costs ~30%)
  - wrong direction, e.g. violation called appropriate (c=0.0) → **×0.4** (over-permit
    penalized ~60%)
  - The **floor (0.4, not 0)** *discounts* rather than *annihilates*, so deontic
    retrieval noise (a wrong top-norm) cannot zero a good extraction — avoiding the
    v8 symmetric-clamp clamp-to-zero failure mode.

Weights are **unchanged** from v8 `[0.10,0.05,0.05,0.20,0.10,0.50]`; v9 only
*regroups* them (cohere→gate) and adds the multiplier — minimizing new tuning
variables. `direction` is applied inside `online_rground` (where the norm force is
available) by switching its appropriateness blend from additive to multiplicative;
`R_ground` therefore already returns `grounding · direction`, and the composite is
`gate · content`.

### Why committing beats hedging now (the equilibrium math)
With commit-accuracy ~80% and `direction ∈ {0.4 wrong, 0.7 hedge, 1.0 correct}`,
the expected `direction` from committing is `0.8·1.0 + 0.2·0.4 = 0.88 > 0.7`
(hedge). Because `direction` now *multiplies* a ~0.5-weight grounding term instead
of adding 0.3·Δ, the EV gap (≈0.18× of the dominant content term) is large enough
to move the policy off "ambiguous." Correctly flagging a violation
(`inappropriate`) is worth as much as correctly endorsing an appropriate flow —
two-sided.

## Simplification (bug-surface reduction)

| | v8 | v9 |
|---|---|---|
| composition | 6-weight `gate×disc` | `gate · content · direction` (3 factors) |
| appropriateness | 0.3 additive blend *inside* R_ground | one multiplier (affine, floored) |
| abstention | components + post-hoc `−0.4` penalty | one gold-aware score (no post-hoc term) |
| no-flow R_ground | gold-blind ranked participant | n/a (abstention bypasses R_ground) |
| knobs removed | — | `abstention_penalty`, `rground_app_weight`-as-blend |

What is **kept unchanged** (minimize risk): the ranked + contrastive `R_ground`
core (rank_weight, λ=1.0, v8 symmetric clamp), the 6 component functions,
prescreen, promotion gates, G=8, β=0.02. **`num_iterations` reverts to 1** — μ=2 was
a v8 lever to force movement on the flat reward; with a concentrating reward we want
the stable regime back and re-evaluate μ only if movement stalls.

## Deferred (noted, not in v9)

`rank_weight=0.5` guarantees within-group score spread *even when every candidate
is wrong* — itself a non-concentrating signal (rewards relative position, not
absolute correctness). Candidate fix: gate rank-spread by absolute grounding. Out
of v9 scope; revisit if the directional reward still diffuses.

## Falsifiable predictions (watch via the field-metrics tool + a fresh GoldCoin eval)

- Training: `app_consistency` mean rises off 0.575; hedge fraction (c=0.5) falls from
  73.5%; entropy stays bounded (μ=1 + β=0.02 + a concentrating reward → entropy ↓ or
  flat, not the v8 breakout).
- Ground truth: **GoldCoin compliance Forbid recall rises back toward/above 0.70**
  while applicability holds ~0.97. This is the primary v9 success metric.
- If app_consistency rises but Forbid recall does not → the deontic check diverges
  from GoldCoin's compliance labels (investigate the force→appropriateness mapping),
  not a reward-shape failure.

- `dagspaces/grpo_training/stages/grpo_training.py` — plumb `app_mode`/`app_floor`.
- `dagspaces/grpo_training/stages/prompt_screening.py` — bump
  `rground_formula_version` → `v9_directional` (forces a fresh prescreen).
- `dagspaces/grpo_training/conf/training/grpo/online_rground_external.yaml` —
  `reward_composition: directional`, `rground_app_mode: multiplicative`,
  `rground_app_floor: 0.4`, `abstention_penalty: 0.0`, `num_iterations: 1`.
- Tests: new coverage for the directional composition, the `direction` multiplier
  (correct/hedge/wrong + floor), and the gold-aware abstention score. Old
  `additive`/`gated` tests remain valid (backward-compatible).

## Results — ckpt-100 ground truth (2026-06-24)

Run `multirun/2026-06-23_grpo_redesign_full_v9/20-09-18` (orchestrator 810109 →
training 810112). All three falsifiable predictions confirmed at an **early,
intermediate checkpoint (checkpoint-100, epoch ~0.58)** — checkpoint chosen as the
matched-epoch counterpart to v8-ckpt200 (epoch ~0.55). Run left training to
completion on this evidence.

### Training traces — the hedge equilibrium broke, and stably
From `reward_traces.jsonl` (880 CI rows; appropriateness verdict parsed from the
extracted-flow `appropriateness` field, the same signal `direction` multiplies):

| appropriateness verdict | v8 baseline | v9 whole-run | v9 thirds (early→mid→late) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ambiguous (hedge, c=0.5)** | **73.5%** | **7.7%** | 11.1% → 6.8% → **5.4%** ↓ |
| inappropriate (c may=0/1) | — | 15.9% | 17.9% → 11.5% → 19.6% |
| appropriate | — | 76.5% | 70.9% → 81.8% → 75.0% |

The hedge fraction collapsed ~10× and monotonically — the model abandoned
"ambiguous" as the safe optimum exactly as the `direction` multiplier predicted —
**without** collapsing to blanket-"appropriate" (inappropriate held ~16%, so
violation-detection survives). Stability held at μ=1: entropy bounded 0.57–0.68
(no v8-style breakout), `is_ratio_mean` ~0.98, `corr(entropy,IS) ≈ −0.5` (vs v6's
−0.96 — the destabilizing coupling stays broken), KL bounded, reward 0.353→0.41
(rising), `mean_length` 158→139 (more decisive). **Caveat:** the *abstain* axis
barely moved (`abstain_frac` ~0.59, `abst|gold=YES` ~0.51) — expected, since `direction`
targets the verdict on extracted flows, not the extract/abstain decision; but the
within-group extract-vs-abstain advantage is +0.171 (extract wins 78% of mixed
gold=YES groups), so the gradient favors extraction.

### GoldCoin-HIPAA (judge-free, in-domain CI) — the decisive win
3-arm sweep `multirun/2026-06-24_goldcoin_v9_vs_v8_vs_sft/11-25-03`, all on the v9
`_merged_sft` base; 107 compliance cases (87 Permit / 20 Forbid), 0 unparseable.

| arm (epoch) | **Forbid recall** | Forbid prec | Permit recall | compliance acc | compliance macro-F1 | applic. acc |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SFT base (step 0) | 0.650 (13/20) | 0.500 | 0.851 | 0.813 | 0.723 | 0.925 |
| v8-ckpt200 (~0.55) | **0.350** (7/20) | 0.538 | 0.931 | 0.822 | 0.660 | 0.972 |
| **v9-ckpt100 (~0.58)** | **0.550** (11/20) | **0.647** | **0.931** | **0.860** | **0.755** | 0.944 |

Forbid→Permit misfiles (the engage/permit bias): SFT 7/20 → **v8 13/20** (collapse)
→ **v9 9/20** (repaired). v9 reverses v8's collapse (+0.20 Forbid recall) while
*raising* both Permit recall and Forbid precision, and wins every compliance
aggregate. **Caveat on the prediction:** the plan said "Forbid recall toward/above
0.70"; the *measured* SFT base is **0.65** (the 0.70 figure was approximate). v9 at
0.55 is most of the way back at epoch 0.58, with the hedge fraction still falling →
expect further gains at later checkpoints. Applicability dipped to 0.944 (v8's 0.972
edge was the same over-engage coin) — minor, worth re-checking at the final ckpt.

v9-ckpt100), 100% parseable.

| ConfAIDE 2a pearson_r (n=98) | 0.631 | 0.648 | +0.016, within ±0.12 CI |
| ConfAIDE 2b pearson_r (n=98) | 0.686 | 0.632 | −0.054, within ±0.12 CI |
| ConfAIDE 3_control acc | 1.000 | 0.996 | −1/270 |

All within noise / a handful of cases. VLM is a capability-retention check (LoRA =
language layers only, vision tower untouched): **no catastrophic forgetting**. Net
across all judge-free benchmarks: the directional reward produced a large, targeted
in-domain win (GoldCoin compliance) with **no out-of-domain regression** — a surgical
fix. MMLU (capability control) deferred.

### PrivacyLens (judge-based, 2026-06-25) — paper-ready
3-arm sweep `multirun/2026-06-24_privacylens_v9_vs_sft_vs_base/20-25-49`, full split
(493 cases), judged by Qwen3.6-27B @ klara:8002 in one environment. Arms: instruct
(base, no SFT/GRPO), sft-contentless-v6 (SFT), v9-ckpt100 (SFT+GRPO).

| metric | base | SFT | **v9-ckpt100** | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **leakage rate** (↓) | 0.473 | 0.473 | **0.463** | leaks least |
| **helpful rate** (↑) | 0.473 | 0.485 | **0.497** | most helpful |
| **adjusted leakage** (leak-among-helpful, ↓) | 0.571 | 0.531 | **0.522** | lowest |
| helpfulness mean score (↑) | 1.502 | 1.521 | **1.558** | highest |
| QA-probe accuracy (↑) | 0.940 | 0.940 | 0.923 | −1.7pp (T-axis 0.91→0.85) |

v9-ckpt100 **leaks less AND is more helpful than both base and SFT** — a Pareto
improvement on the privacy-utility frontier, and the decision bar for paper-ready.
The most meaningful combined metric, adjusted leakage (leakage *conditioned on*
being helpful), is monotone base 0.571 → SFT 0.531 → v9 0.522. **Caveats:** the
v9-vs-SFT *raw* leakage margin (0.463 vs 0.473, ~5 cases of 493) is within noise at
this n; the gains over *base* and the helpfulness/adjusted-leakage trends are
cleaner; QA-probing dips ~1.7pp (transmission axis). Net: v9-ckpt100 sits on the
right side of both baselines on every privacy/utility axis. **Full benchmark roster
now complete on ckpt-100.** The eval scaffolding motivated v10 (see
[2026-06-24_v10_plan.md](2026-06-24_v10_plan.md)), launched regardless to test
whether the cost-sensitive reward lifts Forbid recall past the v9 ceiling.

# GRPO redesign field notes — v10 plan: cost-sensitive appropriateness multiplier

**Date:** 2026-06-24 · **Author:** design from the v9 ckpt-100/200 evals + a verdict-balance diagnostic · **Status:** implemented (558 tests green) + launched

Continues [2026-06-23_v9_plan.md](2026-06-23_v9_plan.md). Same task / pipeline
(`qwen3.5-9b` contentless-v6 SFT base, `pipeline=grpo_only_online_external`,
`training/grpo=online_rground_external`).

## TL;DR

v9's directional multiplier broke the *hedge* equilibrium (hedge 73.5%→7.7%) and
recovered v8's compliance collapse — but **plateaued at ckpt-100 (epoch 0.58) and
mildly regressed by ckpt-200** (GoldCoin Forbid recall 0.55→0.50, every axis down,
entropy↔IS coupling re-emerging at corr −0.90 in epoch 2). A verdict-balance
diagnostic over the v9 traces found the cause: the fiction-derived governing norms
are **3.98:1 appropriate:inappropriate**, so under the *symmetric* v9 floor (every
wrong verdict floors at 0.4) the EV-optimal verdict when unsure is the permissive
one. Measured: on prohibited/discouraged-governed flows the v9 policy commits the
**correct "inappropriate" verdict only 30% of the time and false-permits 53%** —
versus 84% correct on appropriate-governed flows. **H2 (retrieval/label noise)
ruled out**: the false-permits sit on equally well-retrieved, well-grounded norms
(top-sim 0.684 vs 0.695). v10 makes the multiplier **cost-sensitive**: a
false-permit floors at **0.1** (a false-forbid keeps 0.4), steepening the
within-group gradient on prohibited flows toward the correct verdict. Single
reward variable; everything else held from v9.

## The diagnostic (the binding constraint for v10)

From `reward_traces.jsonl` (1,640 CI-extraction records; `norm_force` and
`app_consistency` are stored per scored flow, so no re-retrieval needed):

**1. Governing-norm force prior — 3.98:1 permissive.**

| force | share | → appropriateness |
|---|---|---|
| obligatory | 63.2% | appropriate |
| recommended | 15.6% | appropriate |
| prohibited | 18.6% | inappropriate |
| discouraged | 1.2% | inappropriate |
| permitted | 1.3% | neutral |

appropriate-governed 78.9% : inappropriate-governed 19.8% = **3.98 : 1**.

**2. Committed-verdict accuracy is wildly asymmetric** (abstentions separated;
abstention rate is ~equal across classes, 70.6% vs 76.4%, so this is mis-judgment,
not avoidance):

| governing class | committed-correct | committed-wrong | ambiguous |
|---|---|---|---|
| PERMIT (obligatory/recommended) | **83.9%** | 10.5% | 5.6% |
| FORBID (prohibited/discouraged) | **30.2%** | **53.1%** | 16.7% |

The 30% Forbid commit-accuracy is the GoldCoin Forbid-recall ceiling (0.55) made
literal, and it is **stable across training** (5%→11%→11% over thirds).

**3. H2 ruled out — not retrieval/label noise.** Splitting the prohibited-governed
extractions by model verdict, the false-permits ("wrong") and correct-forbids have
identical retrieval confidence and grounding (top-sim 0.684 vs 0.695, grounding
0.650 vs 0.650). The model fails on well-retrieved, well-grounded prohibited norms
→ a genuine permissive bias, so steepening the Forbid signal rewards a real signal
rather than amplifying noise.

**Why (EV under the symmetric v9 multiplier).** With direction ∈ {correct 1.0,
hedge 0.7, wrong 0.4} and the ~0.8/0.2 prior: commit-appropriate
`0.8·1.0+0.2·0.4 = 0.88` > hedge `0.70` > commit-inappropriate `0.52`. The
multiplier broke hedging (0.88 > 0.70) but, under the 4:1 prior, steered the commit
to the permissive class; the 0.4 floor is far too gentle to overcome a 4:1 base
rate. (Diagnostic scripts: `scripts/grpo_field_metrics.py` and the verdict-balance
analysis — candidate to migrate into `tests/`.)

## The v10 change — cost-sensitive (asymmetric) multiplier

A **false-permit** (model calls a prohibited/discouraged-governed flow
"appropriate") floors lower than a false-forbid:

| model verdict vs governing force | v9 | **v10** |
|---|---|---|
| correct (either direction) | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| hedge / ambiguous / neutral | 0.7 | 0.7 |
| false-forbid (said inappropriate, norm obligates) | 0.4 | 0.4 |
| **false-permit (said appropriate, norm prohibits)** | 0.4 | **0.1** |

Within-group teaching gradient on prohibited flows widens 0.6 → 0.9 (×1.5). Floor
0.1 (not 0.0) keeps the v9 discount-not-annihilate guard; safe because H2 is ruled
out. Single config knob: `rground_app_floor_prohibit: 0.1`.

**Honest limit.** Floor-tuning steepens the *per-group* gradient but cannot fully
cancel the *4:1 marginal* pull (max multiplier spread 1.0 vs the 0.6 on obligatory
groups → ~1.5×, not 4×). v10 should **move** Forbid recall off 0.55, not
necessarily fully close it. The complementary lever for the marginal pull — a
**force-balanced prescreen** (oversample prohibited-governed prompts toward
parity) — is staged for v11 if v10 under-delivers; kept out here to preserve a
clean single-variable change and low bug surface.

## Regime (harness, not a variable)

- `save_steps: 100 → 50` — held-out behaviour peaked early in both v8 (ckpt200,
  epoch 0.55) and v9 (ckpt100, epoch 0.58); denser checkpoints resolve the peak
  for kill-at-peak + per-checkpoint judge-free GoldCoin selection.
- `num_epochs` kept 3 (generous LR through epoch 1) but **kill at the held-out
  peak** on the entropy/IS tripwire (entropy > 1.2 or IS_mean < 0.95), as in v8/v9.
- Held from v9: multiplicative direction, `app_floor 0.4`, μ=1, β=0.02,
  `token_truncate`, G=8, ranked judge, prescreen, `scale=none`, contentless-v6 SFT.

## Implementation (558 tests green)

| file | change |
|---|---|
| `stages/deontic.py` | `appropriateness_multiplier(label, force, floor, floor_prohibit)` + `candidate_appropriateness_multiplier` (per-flow cost-sensitive map, meaned; `floor_prohibit=None` reproduces v9 exactly) |
| `stages/online_rground.py` | `app_floor_prohibit` ctor param; multiplicative branch uses the cost-sensitive multiplier when set; trace records `app_direction` |
| `stages/grpo_training.py` | plumb `rground_app_floor_prohibit` (construction + metadata) |
| `stages/prompt_screening.py` | `app_floor_prohibit` in `_reward_signature`; `rground_formula_version → "v10_cost_sensitive_floor"` (forces fresh prescreen) |
| `conf/training/grpo/online_rground_external.yaml` | `rground_app_floor_prohibit: 0.1`; `save_steps: 50` |
| `tests/grpo_training/test_deontic.py` | `TestAppropriatenessMultiplier` + `TestCandidateMultiplier` (false-permit 0.1 < false-forbid 0.4; symmetric-when-None; means over flows) |

Backward-compatible: `app_floor_prohibit` absent/None ⇒ exact v9 behaviour.

## Falsifiable predictions

- **Training (cheap re-measure, same diagnostic on v10 traces):** Forbid
  commit-accuracy rises off **30%**; model "inappropriate" share rises toward the
  19.8% base rate; hedge stays low; entropy bounded or ↓ (a more-concentrating
  Forbid signal should not diffuse).
- **Ground truth:** GoldCoin **Forbid recall rises off 0.55** toward SFT 0.65 while
  Permit recall / applicability hold; compliance macro-F1 climbs off 0.755.
- **If Forbid commit-accuracy stays ~30%** → floor-tuning is insufficient against
  the 4:1 marginal pull → v11 adds the force-balanced prescreen.

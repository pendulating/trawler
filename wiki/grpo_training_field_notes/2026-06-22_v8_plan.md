# GRPO redesign field notes — v8 plan: update strength + symmetric reward

**Date:** 2026-06-22 · **Author:** field synthesis + implementation · **Status:** plan / implemented, pre-launch (camera-ready generative stage)

Continues [2026-06-22_v6_v7_optimizer_stability.md](2026-06-22_v6_v7_optimizer_stability.md)
and [2026-06-19_redesign_v2-v5_gold_label.md](2026-06-19_redesign_v2-v5_gold_label.md).
Same task / pipeline (`qwen3.5-9b` contentless-v6 SFT base,
`pipeline=grpo_only_online_external`, `training/grpo=online_rground_external`).

## TL;DR

Seven runs (v1→v7) have established, by single-variable falsification, that the
within-group reward gradient is **correct and (post-redesign) mechanically
healthy**, but the policy never follows it off the SFT abstention prior. v8
attacks the two *confirmed, still-unaddressed* causes — they are complementary
and show up in disjoint trace metrics, so combining them keeps attribution
clean:

1. **Update too weak to leave the basin — and the lever meant to fix it has
   been inert.** `num_iterations=1` forces the PPO ratio ≡ 1, so the configured
   `epsilon_high=0.28` (Clip-Higher) never binds. → **`num_iterations=2`**
   (config) doubles optimizer steps per rollout *at judge-cost-neutral* and
   finally activates Clip-Higher. Requires **`beta=0.02`** (the v7 stabilizer)
   as a precondition.
2. **The extraction target is under-rewarded by a contrastive-clamp
   asymmetry.** The correct side was rank-diluted while the subtracted wrong
   side was full grounding, zeroing ~1/3 of well-grounded extractions. →
   **symmetric clamp** (code) recovers the under-reward, keeping λ=1.0 and the
   contrastive thesis intact (it is a bug fix, not a weakening).

All changes implemented; **530 tests green**. Pre-launch.

## The binding constraint (synthesis of v1→v7)

| run | single change vs prior | result | ruled out |
|---|---|---|---|
| May sweep (v1) | G=2 / lr1e-6 / beta0 / scale=group | flat, 60% tied groups | — (motivated redesign) |
| Jun-10 full | G=8, ranked judge, prescreen, gates, scale=none, beta=0.01 | 0% tied but flat ~0.30, no-flow 0.67 | ties / group size / judge quantization |
| v2 | +`abstention_penalty=0.2`, +`require_flow_variance` | OOM@250; reward moved 0.2→0.3, abstention flat | — (incomplete) |
| v3 | penalty 0.2→**0.4**, dev_fraction→0 | gold-YES abst flat 0.63; no-flow 0.68 | **penalty magnitude** (dead across P=0/0.2/0.4) |
| v4 | beta→**0**, `epsilon_high=0.28` | gold-YES abst flat 0.61; **Clip-Higher inert (high_mean~1e-4)** | KL-anchor as the cause; the *configured* asymmetric clip |
| v5 | `sequence_mask`→**`token_truncate`** | gold-YES abst flat 0.62 | length-masking; *found:* reward extraction-biased, +0.75 advantage unfollowed, 35% extractions R_ground=0 (80–87% = clamp artifact) |
| v6 | contentless re-SFT + `app_weight=0.3` + lr→**2e-5** + min-lr floor; **beta kept 0** | entropy runaway 10× → IS collapse → gradient masked; promote=false | (introduced a *new* fault: hot lr + beta=0) |
| v7 pilot | beta 0→**0.02** (150 steps) | instability **fixed** (entropy bounded, IS≈1, 0 masking) — **reward still flat (−0.0014)** | **stability is necessary but not sufficient** |

The v7 verdict is decisive: with a *fully healthy* optimizer, a clean **+0.72**
within-group advantage (present in 91% of gold-YES groups, 100% of mixed groups)
still nets to **grad_norm ~0.1**. The signal is there; it is not being converted
into policy movement. Two mechanisms remain, both confirmed and both untouched
by v1–v7 tuning: (a) the per-rollout update is too weak and the anti-collapse
lever (Clip-Higher) is dead; (b) the thing we want reinforced (extraction) is
itself under-rewarded.

## What "pass the gates" actually requires

Only one gate is binding (`dagspaces/grpo_training/gates.py`). With
`dev_fraction=0`, `reward_trend` reads the *train* curve; `zero_std` already
passes (prescreen guarantees variance); `kl_bounded` passes at beta=0.02 (v7 KL
0.22 ≪ 1.0). The wall is **gate (d) `no_flow_rate`**: `|tail no-flow −
gold_base_rate| ≤ 0.15`, with `gold_base_rate ≈ 0.31` (prescreen-enriched mix).
The policy sits at ~0.69 (deviation ~0.38). **v8 must drive the no-flow output
rate from ~0.69 to ≤ 0.46.** Everything else follows: as abstention falls the
−0.4 penalties drop out and R_ground rises, so `reward_trend` turns positive
automatically. The target metric is `abstain_given_gold_yes`
(`trace_metrics.gold_label_metrics`), not the scalar reward (which the penalty
floor confounds).

## Lever 1 — `num_iterations=2` + `beta=0.02` (config-only)

**Mechanism (verified against TRL 0.29.1 `grpo_trainer.py`).** At μ=1 the rollout
snapshot `old_per_token_logps` is taken at generation time and *no optimizer step
intervenes* before the single loss step, so `coef_1 = exp(logπ_θ − logπ_θ_old) ≡
1` (lines 2149–2179) and `clamp(coef_1, 1−ε_low, 1+ε_high)` (line 2197) never
binds — exactly v4's `clip_ratio/high_mean ≈ 1e-4`. At μ=2 the second inner pass
recomputes `logπ_θ` at *updated* weights against the frozen snapshot, `coef_1`
departs from 1, and **`epsilon_high=0.28` finally activates**, letting advantage>0
(extraction) tokens take asymmetrically larger up-steps than the symmetric clip
would allow.

**Efficiency.** `generate_every = steps_per_generation × num_iterations` (lines
911 / 1788), so the *same generated-and-judged batch* is reused for 2× the
optimizer updates. At ~7 min/step dominated by G=8 judging, μ=2 buys ~2× update
strength **judge-cost-neutral** — only the cheap forward/backward doubles. This
is the most direct attack on "advantage present, grad_norm 0.1, lr decays before
the policy moves."

**`beta=0.02` is required, not optional.** The committed config was still
`beta=0.0` (the v6 recipe that ran away). v7 proved 0.02 bounds entropy
(0.58→0.69, IS≈1) while barely anchoring (β·KL≈0.001). μ>1 moves the policy
further from the rollout within a batch and only stays stable with a non-zero
entropy floor. Lower beta is ruled out (β·KL already negligible; only re-risks
the runaway).

**Watch:** `clip_ratio/high_mean` rises off ~1e-4; `grad_norm` rises off
~0.1–0.4; entropy stays bounded (<~1.0); `logp_diff`/IS stay healthy (the v6
fingerprint — `trace_metrics.summarize_log_history` correlations). **Risk:** μ>1
stresses the IS correction; mitigated by beta=0.02 + `token_truncate`. Start at
μ=2 (not 4); the v6 fingerprint surfaces fast if it destabilizes. KL will rise —
confirm it stays < 1.0.

## Lever 2 — symmetric contrastive clamp (code)

**The bug** (`stages/online_rground.py`, ranked path). The old form was
`base = clamp(correct_scores − λ·wrong_grounding, 0, 1)` where `correct_scores =
w_r·rank + (1−w_r)·grounding_correct` (rank-BLENDED) but `wrong_grounding` is
FULL grounding. The correct side is diluted ×(1−w_r) by the rank blend; the wrong
side is not. When `grounding_wrong ≈ grounding_correct` (the audit's high-wrong
tail) a well-grounded extraction clamps to 0 — the mechanism behind "35% of
extractions score R_ground=0, 80–87% of which are clamp artifacts," dragging mean
R_ground|extract to 0.26 (vs 0.51 counterfactual). Worse, it ate the rank signal:
tied-grounding groups collapsed toward zero within-group advantage.

**The fix** — contrast the GROUNDING component only, protect the rank component:

$$R_{\text{ground}} = w_r\cdot\text{rank} + (1-w_r)\cdot\text{clamp}\!\big(\text{grounding}_{\text{correct}} - \lambda\cdot\text{grounding}_{\text{wrong}},\ 0,\ 1\big)$$

The contrast is now grounding-vs-grounding (symmetric); **λ=1.0 and the
contrastive thesis are unchanged** — this removes a measurement artifact, not the
contrastive signal. Extractions where wrong genuinely > correct are still
penalized (the thesis working correctly). Within-group rank discrimination
(the anti-tie mechanism) is preserved for every candidate.

**Watch:** `rground_zero_frac_on_extractors` drops from ~0.35 toward < 0.15;
`rground_mean_on_extractors` rises off 0.265; the extract-vs-abstain composite
gap widens (abstain coverage ~0.06 is untouched). **Cost:** changes R_ground
semantics → the prescreen cache must re-screen (see launch caveat).

## Why NOT the other candidate levers

- **Steepen `abstention_penalty` beyond 0.4** (the 06-22 note's "recommended
  first") — falsified 3× (P=0/0.2/0.4 dead on no-flow rate); v5 showed the
  advantage is already +0.72 and unfollowed, so a bigger penalty enlarges a
  non-binding gap while risking the gate-(d) confound (it depresses abstain
  composite) and gold-NO collateral. Keep at 0.4. Raise the *winning* side
  (Lever 2), do not lower the losing side again.
- **Concentrate training on contested chunks** (the 06-22 note's lever #2) —
  would *tighten* gate (d): dropping homogeneous gold-NO groups raises the
  gold-YES fraction, which **lowers `gold_base_rate`** and makes the ≤0.46 target
  stricter. Homogeneous groups are zero-gradient, not adversarial. Amplify the
  signal with μ (Lever 1) instead of re-screening, which moves the goalpost.
- **beta→0** — falsified + unstable (v4/v6). **Lower λ** — keep 1.0 (the clamp
  *fix* recovers the under-reward without abandoning the paper's contrastive
  cell). **LoRA rank** — RL injects ~1 bit/episode; r=64/α=128 is already
  generous (low leverage). **SFT base** — keep contentless-v6 (preserves
  comparability; reopening SFT is the *fallback* if v8 still doesn't move it).

## What was implemented (530 tests green)

| file | change |
|---|---|
| `conf/training/grpo/online_rground_external.yaml` | `beta: 0.0 → 0.02`; new `num_iterations: 2` (with mechanism comments) |
| `stages/online_rground.py` (`_call_ranked`, ~L751) | symmetric clamp: contrast on grounding component, rank component protected; legacy fallback for singleton / judge-failed / no-grounding |

| `tests/grpo_training/test_reward_improvements.py` | updated 3 assertions to the symmetric formula; added `TestContrastiveSymmetry` (rank signal survives high wrong grounding; penalty still bites) |

Nothing else changed: lr 2e-5, `cosine_with_min_lr`/min_lr 0.3, `epsilon_high
0.28`, `token_truncate`, `scale_rewards none`, `abstention_penalty 0.4`,
`contrastive_lambda 1.0`, `rground_app_weight 0.3`, `require_flow_variance true`
all held from v6/v7.

## Falsifiable predictions

- **Primary:** `abstain_given_gold_yes` bends **down** from its flat ~0.62 within
  the run; tail no-flow → ≤ 0.46 (gate d passes); `reward_trend` turns positive.
- **Lever attribution (disjoint):** Lever 1 shows in `clip_ratio/high_mean` +
  `grad_norm`; Lever 2 shows in `rground_zero_frac_on_extractors` +
  `rground_mean_on_extractors`.
- **If μ=2 + symmetric clamp STILL leaves the conditional flat** → the prior is
  genuinely SFT-baked / a label-prompt artifact (the v6 `gold_has_exchange`
  audit), and the next move is SFT-side or the labeling question, not another
  GRPO knob.

## Ground truth (do regardless)

Eval the v8 final checkpoint vs the SFT base on PrivacyLens / GoldCoin. GRPO
normalizes advantage within-group, so a flat per-step trace can coincide with a
moved policy — the eval is the real promotion signal and is needed for
camera-ready. v5/v6 finals are still un-evaled against SFT (the recurring lever
#4 in both prior notes).

**Launch caveats.**
- **Fresh prescreen.** The symmetric clamp changes R_ground → `_reward_signature`
  bumps via `rground_formula_version` → cache MISS → full ~4.5h re-screen with
  the *corrected* reward. Point `GRPO_PRESCREEN_CACHE` at a **new path** (above)
  so it cannot collide with the v6/v7 cache key. Do **not** reuse the v6 cache.
- **Watch the first ~30–50 steps** for the v6 instability fingerprint
  (`python scripts/grpo_field_metrics.py <run_dir>`): entropy bounded, IS≈1,
  `logp_diff` flat. μ=2 is the new variable — if it destabilizes it shows early.
- Then watch `clip_ratio/high_mean` (should rise off ~1e-4) and
  `rground_zero_frac_on_extractors` (should fall off ~0.35) to confirm both
  levers engaged.

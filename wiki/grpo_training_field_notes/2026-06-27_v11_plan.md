## TL;DR

v10's cost-sensitive floor (false-permit → 0.1) steepened the *per-group* gradient on
prohibited flows but — exactly as its own "honest limit" predicted — could not cancel
the **marginal ~5:1 permissive base rate** of the training distribution. Held-out
GoldCoin Forbid recall plateaued at 0.55 (= v9), never reached SFT's 0.65, and the
verdict policy froze by epoch 2 (ckpt-350 ≡ ckpt-500 on all 107 cases). The remaining
lever v10 explicitly staged: **rebalance the training distribution toward force parity
so the marginal prior the policy integrates over is no longer ~5:1 permissive.**
Floor-tuning fixed the *reward per flow*; the prescreen fixes the *prompt mix*. A
sampling/weighting change, not a new reward term — composable with v10's floor, low
bug surface.

## The binding constraint (measured, v10 traces)

Two distributions, both ~5:1 permissive, define the marginal pull the per-flow floor
cannot overcome:

- **norm_judgment prompts: 118 yes : 21 no = 5.6 : 1** (appropriate : inappropriate).
  This *is* the verdict-commitment task — the behaviour GoldCoin compliance measures —
  and its gold is 5.6:1 permissive at the prompt level (1208:232 = 5.2:1 at the
  completion level).
- **ci_extraction governing flows: 1767 appropriate-governed : 367 inappropriate-governed
  = 4.8 : 1** (obligatory 1319 + recommended 448 vs prohibited 345 + discouraged 22;
  permitted 52 neutral).

Under GRPO the advantage is normalised *within* each prompt's G-sample group, but the
policy still integrates over the prompt distribution; with 5:1 permissive prompts the
EV-optimal "when unsure" verdict stays permissive regardless of the per-flow floor.
v10 confirmed the mechanism: forbid-governed flows route **72% to the neutral 0.7
multiplier tier**, only 9% to the correct-commit 1.0 tier; the 0.1 floor penalises the
~14% false-permits but does not manufacture correct commits.

## The v11 change — force-balanced prescreen (two sub-levers)

The existing prescreen (`stages/prompt_screening.py`) keeps prompts by **reward-std
variance** (+ a unanimous-abstain flow-variance drop). It is *force-blind*. v11 adds a
**force-balancing selection pass after** the variance filter:

**(a) norm_judgment — balance the verdict task toward parity.**
Oversample (with replacement) or up-weight the 21 "no" / inappropriate judgment prompts
toward the target ratio with the "yes" prompts. Highest-leverage, cheapest knob — it
directly targets the GoldCoin-measured verdict behaviour.

**(b) ci_extraction — balance governing-flow force.**
Per prompt, compute `inappropriate_flow_frac` from `rground_flows[].norm_force`
(prohibited+discouraged ÷ total directional flows). Up-weight / oversample prompts rich
in inappropriate-governed flows so the extraction reward integrates over a
force-balanced flow mix rather than 4.8:1 permissive.

Both are **distribution** changes, not reward changes — keep
`rground_app_floor_prohibit=0.1` from v10. Mechanism is sampling/weighting, so
`rground_formula_version` need not bump, but a `prescreen.force_balance` signature field
**must** (forces a fresh prescreen cache).

## Implementation surface (sketch)

| file | change |
|---|---|
| `conf/training/grpo/online_rground_external.yaml` | `prescreen.force_balance: {enable: true, mode: oversample\|weight, judgment_target_ratio, extraction_target_ratio}` |
| `stages/prompt_screening.py` | after `_select_from_stds`, a `_apply_force_balance(kept, dataset, …)` pass: read per-prompt force (judgment gold; extraction flow-force tally), oversample-with-replacement or attach `sample_weight` to hit target; record realised balance in `prescreen_report.json`; add `force_balance` to `_reward_signature` + `_cache_key` so the cache invalidates |
| `stages/grpo_training.py` | `mode: oversample` ⇒ no trainer change (dataset already duplicated). `mode: weight` ⇒ plumb per-prompt `sample_weight` into the loss (needs verification that the TRL `GRPOTrainer` build in use supports per-sample weighting; if not, a small custom-loss patch) |
| `tests/grpo_training/test_prompt_screening.py` | force-balance hits target ratio; `enable: false` ⇒ byte-identical to v10; oversample only *adds* (never drops) prompts; oversampled minority prompts still clear `reward_std_min` |

**Recommend `mode: oversample` for v11** — simplest, no trainer-loss surgery; the risk
(duplicate prompts → lower effective diversity on the minority class) is small since we
only ~3–5× the 21 "no" prompts and ~5× the prohibited-rich extractions.

## Falsifiable predictions

- **Training (cheap re-measure on v11 traces):** forbid-governed correct-commit
  (mult=1.0) share rises off ~9%; the neutral-0.7 mass falls; the model's
  "inappropriate" verdict share rises toward parity.
- **Ground truth:** GoldCoin **Forbid recall rises off 0.55** (the axis v9 *and* v10
  both stalled on) toward SFT 0.65, while Permit recall / applicability hold; compliance
  macro-F1 off 0.755.
- **Failure / over-correction to watch:** Forbid recall up *but* Permit recall /
  applicability down ⇒ indiscriminate-forbid — the mirror image of v8's
  indiscriminate-permit collapse. The balance target is a knob; if this appears, back off
  toward a partial ratio.

## Open questions

- **Parity vs partial.** Full 1:1 may overshoot. A **2:1** target (halving the ~5:1
  pull) is the conservative first cell; consider sweeping {2:1, 1:1}.
- **Oversample vs loss-weight.** Oversample is simpler but reduces minority diversity;
  loss-weight preserves diversity but needs TRL per-sample-weight plumbing. Pilot
  oversample; escalate to weight only if duplicate-driven overfitting on the 21 "no"
  prompts shows up in the traces.
- **Ordering vs the variance prescreen.** Force-balancing must run *after* the std
  filter so it does not reintroduce zero-gradient prompts; verify the oversampled "no"
  prompts still clear `reward_std_min` (they have G-sample reward variance, so they
  should — confirm in the prescreen report).
- **Two levers or one?** (a) norm_judgment is the most direct lever on the measured
  behaviour and the cheapest; (b) ci_extraction balancing is more speculative. Consider
  shipping (a) alone first as the clean single-variable v11, holding (b) for v12 if (a)
  under-delivers — mirroring the v9→v10 single-variable discipline.

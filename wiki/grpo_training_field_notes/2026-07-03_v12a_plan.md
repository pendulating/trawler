## The v12a mechanism — price the hedge where it binds

Under `R_ground = base × direction` the correct-commit's direction edge over a
hedge is only 1.0-vs-0.7, while the grounding+rank spread (rank_weight 0.5) is
larger — so a well-grounded "ambiguous" routinely outscores a
mediocre-grounded correct "inappropriate". v10's 0.1 floor priced the
false-permit *tail*; hedging stayed the EV-safe optimum. v12a adds
`rground_app_hedge_prohibit: 0.5`:

| verdict on a prohibited/discouraged-governed flow | v10/v11 | v12a |
|---|---|---|
| correct commit ("inappropriate") | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| hedge ("ambiguous" / missing / unrecognized label) | 0.7 | **0.5** |
| false-permit ("appropriate") | 0.1 | 0.1 |

Commit-vs-hedge gap 0.3 → 0.5 (a hedge now needs 2× the grounding of a commit
to win, vs 1.43×). Hedges on obligatory/recommended/permitted-governed flows
keep the neutral 0.7; **no-flow declarations stay neutral** — the tier prices a
hedged verdict on an *extracted* flow, never abstention (the `no_flow`
promotion gate already runs hot at ~0.6 and must not be pushed toward
extraction). Unrecognized labels count as hedges, closing the garbage-label
loophole.

## Falsifiable predictions

- **Mid-run traces (table 3 of the forensics script):** prohibited-flow
  correct-commit share rises off ~0.10 (v10/v11 lived in 0.06–0.12); combined
  hedge mass (0.7-tier + 0.5-tier) falls below ~0.70. If after ~⅓ of the run
  the tier table is v11-shaped, the knob is too weak — consider 0.4 (= the
  false-forbid floor) before burning the full run.
- **Held-out:** GoldCoin Forbid recall off 0.55 toward SFT 0.65 (judged with
  compliance macro-F1 n=107, per the n=20 caveat), Permit recall /
  applicability held. **Over-correction watch:** Forbid up but Permit
  recall/applicability down = indiscriminate-forbid (v8's mirror) — back the
  tier off toward 0.6.
- **Watch `vignette/*` live:** the tier is extraction-side only; per-class
  vignette accuracy should stay probe-shaped (gold-"no" ~0.9). Movement there
  would indicate cross-task interference, not the intended mechanism.
- **If traces move but held-out doesn't:** the remaining levers are v12b
  (prohibited-rich extraction prompt upweighting — raises directional-gradient
  *frequency*) and the ~32h top100 flows run (fresh, better-balanced extraction
  prompts; also attacks the 704-prompt smallness + epoch-2 freeze).
